📖 NAVIGATOR'S LOGBOOK GUIDE

Welcome aboard! This logbook tracks every single episode of One Piece from Episode 1 through the latest Elbaph releases and automatically checks the public Jikan & AniList APIs for newly aired episodes.

Episode Classification Key
  • CANON — Directly adapts Eiichiro Oda's manga storyline (including core Amazon Lily Ep. 408–417 and Impel Down Ep. 422–425 & 430–452). Essential viewing.
  • MIXED — Adapts canonical manga chapters or cover-page serials blended with anime-original extensions/recaps (such as the Straw Hat Separation cover stories in Amazon Lily Ep. 418–421 and Impel Down Ep. 453–456, or Levely Ep. 878–889). Vital story context—included in the Catch-Up route.
  • FILLER — 100% anime-original side stories or recap specials (such as Warship Island Ep. 54–60, G-8 Ep. 196–206, Boss Luffy Ep. 406–407, and Little East Blue Ep. 426–429). Skipping all 77 pure filler episodes saves ~30.8 hours!
